Ask a question, or invite us to speak

We welcome questions about the scattered tribes of Israel, the peoples on our map, and the Institute's symposiums and library. We also travel to speak at conferences, houses of worship of every faith, universities, and small gatherings in people's homes, in person or online.

Where we speak

  • Conferences and symposiumsAcademic and community gatherings on history, epic, migration and identity.
  • Houses of worshipSynagogues, churches, mosques, temples and fellowships, for any community centered on Jesus or curious about Israel's story among the nations.
  • Universities and schoolsGuest lectures and seminars on oral traditions and comparative religion.
  • Circles of friendsLiving-room evenings, book groups and round tables for a handful of people.
  • OnlineVideo talks and interviews for groups, podcasts and channels anywhere.
