Symposiums and round tables

Since 2006 the Institute’s researchers and their partners have brought together scholars, community leaders and the peoples themselves to present their traditions and the evidence for an Israelite origin. Most are published as books.

Papers

December 11–12, 2024
Plasa, Meghalaya, India

Scientific Symposium of the United Mikir's Research Society & Lost Tribes Institute

A joint symposium with the United Mikir's Research Society on the Israelite origin of the Mikir/Karbi people of Assam and Meghalaya. Papers covered the Mikir monoliths, their ceremonies and feasts compared with Israel's, and the Humble King Sot Recho. The reciter Longsing Bey performed the Moserah, the Mikir oral history. The Institute's written analysis concludes that the Mikir/Karbi are Israelite in origin.
