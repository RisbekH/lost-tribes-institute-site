Lost Tribes Institute

Tracing the scattered tribes of Israel among the nations

The Lost Tribes Institute is a network of scholars working with indigenous nations and other peoples who say they descend from the tribes of Israel's northern kingdom, exiled in the 8th century BC. Their traditions often carry biblical stories and customs of their own. The Institute organizes round tables and symposiums across Asia and publishes what is presented.

Nations and peoples who say they come from ancient Israel

Communities, synagogues, churches, researchers, writers, university departments and YouTube channels around the world argue that a particular people descends from the ancient tribes of Israel. The register collects those claims, from rabbinic recognitions and old travel accounts to self-published books and online video, and puts each one on the map with the reasons given and the people who give them.

The promised return

Read the chapters →

The term “Lost Tribes” never appears in Scripture. What the Prophets do say, many times, is that the scattered northern tribes will one day come back, and that the houses of Israel and Judah will be joined again.
